Walls first.
Then everything around them.

Vibracrete is precast concrete: posts are set in the ground and ribbed panels slot in between. It goes up quickly, costs less than brick and asks almost nothing of you once it’s built. We supply it, install it and look after it.

New vibracrete walls

Full supply and installation of precast boundary walls from 1.8 m to 2.7 m high, with plain posts or pillars. Panels are made with quality cement and steel and finished neat, so the wall looks as solid as it is.

  • Homes, complexes, schools and farms
  • Factories, yards and church grounds
  • Built to SANS standards
  • Most walls installed in days, not weeks

Extensions

Raise an existing wall with extra panels for more privacy and better security, matched to what’s already there.

Repairs

Cracked panels replaced, leaning sections straightened and loose posts reset so the wall stands true again.

Second-hand walls

Good used panels for a lower-cost boundary, installed to exactly the same standard as new.

Painted finishes

A painted vibracrete wall gives a clean, finished look that suits the house instead of looking like a building site.

Wall spikes

Steel spikes fitted along the top of new or existing walls to stop anyone climbing over.

Maintenance

Regular checks and small fixes that keep posts upright and panels sound for years to come.

From first message to finished wall

No deposit and no paperwork maze. Here’s what happens after you get in touch.

  1. Send us a message

    WhatsApp or call Innocent with your area and roughly how long the wall needs to be.

  2. Free site quote

    We come out, measure up and give you a clear price per metre, with no obligation.

  3. Installation

    Our team digs the footings, sets the posts and slots in the panels, usually within a few days.

  4. Pay when it’s up

    You walk the wall, you’re happy, then you pay. It’s also covered by our 12-month guarantee.

Why people choose vibracrete

Security
A solid 1.8–2.7 m concrete boundary keeps people out and gives you a proper base for electric fencing.
Privacy
No gaps, no see-through. Your garden and driveway stay your business.
Cost
Far cheaper per metre than brick and plaster, and much quicker to put up.
Low upkeep
Weatherproof concrete that doesn’t rot, rust or need painting unless you want it to.
