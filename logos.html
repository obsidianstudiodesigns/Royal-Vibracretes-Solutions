Logo concepts

Four directions for Royal Vibracretes Solutions, built around the product itself: precast posts, ribbed panels and the crown in the name. Each is shown on light, dark and concrete so you can judge it on vans, shirts, signboards and social posts. The website currently uses concept one.

1. The Crown Wall

The crown is made of the wall: panels stacked between posts, with the points capped in brass. Reads as royal at a distance and as vibracrete up close.
